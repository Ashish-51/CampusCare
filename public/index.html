Incident Lifecycle

Transparent Operational Progression

CampusCare enforces a strict lifecycle path to ensure no ticket is ever lost or ignored.

01

Report

Student raises a complaint with descriptive details and photo attachments.

02

Review

Administration triages the incident and evaluates priority levels.

03

Assign

Official maintenance team or service lead is dispatched to investigate.

04

Resolve

Service lead completes work, updating the audit trail log with remarks.

05

Feedback

Student rates resolution satisfaction, formally closing the lifecycle loop.
