Powerful Features

Everything You Need for Campus Maintenance

Streamlining communication between students, hostel wardens, IT leads, and maintenance engineers.

Drag & Drop Photo Attachment

Upload clear photo evidence of broken fixtures, plumbing leaks, or IT hardware issues directly from mobile or desktop.

Step-by-Step Interactive Timeline

Monitor real-time resolution stages: Submitted $\rightarrow$ In Progress $\rightarrow$ Resolved with transparent admin audit notes.

Admin Analytics & Chart.js

Interactive metric counters, category distribution doughnut charts, and departmental workload graphs for management leads.

Post-Resolution Star Ratings

Students can rate the quality of resolution (1-5 stars) and provide feedback to ensure service standards.

Role-Based Security & Protection

Built with Firebase Auth and Cloud Firestore RBAC security rules ensuring data privacy between students and admins.

Instant Search & Multi-Filters
